**<ins>Chapter 1 - The Bone Bottom<ins>**

I feel horrible. Those veiled bugs, the kidnapping from Hallownest…

The little Ghost’s empty mask.

It’s all too much.

Clink.
Clank,
Clang.

My needle! It nearly impaled me in the mask, goodness.
May as well bind with it, then.

I look around. A fairly peaceful area, covered in moss and greenery. In stark contrast to the White Palace, at least.

‘How did I get myself here? Should I even try to figure out, or do I take the easy path out?’ I mutter. I ponder. I don’t have time to waste. Ok, Hornet. Do a Silk Soar. I push my needle to the ground, crouch down, and try to summon Silk around me.

Nothing happens.

It’s as if I’ve forgotten everything I’d spent years training at Hallownest with. I test it out. I can’t summon Silk to perform a Silk Spear.
Even my signature Thread Storm is gone. I try to taunt whoever is around, but I can’t produce any new Silk from my shell. The only thing I can do is bind.

‘Am I so weak I can’t handle a single small concussion anymore?’ I begin to cut through the vines around me as I walk around this lush area. At least I can still swing around my needle. It is Pure Hivesteel, sharpened to a deadly edge, sharper than even the Nail of my dear brother the Hollow Knight.

I look at my perfect needle.
It’s devastated. Ruined. Tarnished. My most treasured gift, from the Queen of the Hive, Vespa herself. Blunted.

I hate this so much.

I keep walking, and find small, mossy , slightly spiky creatures. Quite sharp spikes, after closer inspection. Closer inspection being, trying to pet one and having my palm punctured by a handful of thorns. I’m stupid, of course it would hurt, they’re spikes. Has Father’s stupid saw-blade obsession taught me nothing? I stab my needle into the thing in rage. It is visibly wounded, but my pure needle would have decimated it.

I stab again, and again. I feel the silk build up in me as I kill more of these mossy things. Good for me, bad for them.
‘Have my silk glands been so badly damaged I can only get silk through death and violence?’

As I leave the room, I feel dizzy. That has to be karma for what I just did back there.
I nearly black out, but straighten myself back up.

A gap to cross. Surely it can’t be too difficult. I jump.
And I miss.
And fall.
I catch myself on a ledge right next to a string of something. A string of red things. I pick it up. The string is frayed and worn, but the red things are shiny. I pick it up. Very shiny, and smooth.

I remember.
The shininess of the Radiance’s orange infection, how it shimmered, a false light that only sought to destroy Hallownest.

Enough of these thoughts. The Radiance has been dead since the Little Ghost’s sacrifice. I hope he will one day repay me in some way for me steering him in the right path. He definitely owes me one after that, else he would’ve replaced Hollow.
I pocket the strange beads, even though they make me shiver when I see them.

I get back up to that jump after a lot of clambering, and make it this time. Excellent. I enter a new room. This is different. Hollow. The ground, walls, and ceiling. Skeletal. This place is the bottom of the world, and it seems very bony. I christen the place ‘Bone Bottom’.

I see a door, and try and sprint to it. Except I can’t. Because my dense brain forgot how to run.
The door looks like my mother’s mask. Herrah’s mask.

The little ghost, looking at me, asking consent to execute her without a voice. Because he was the one with no voice to cry suffering. Not Hollow. Not after that scream that called the children of the Abyss. I told him that I would let him do the deed, as long as he saved our kingdom, but I would not watch him do it. And I left the room to let my mother die.

The door seems to be operated by a mechanism that only opens using a harp. I try to prise the door open with my needle. It groans, and I realise it’s going to snap if I keep trying. So I make a mental note, and leave for now.
The next room is simply a hallway, and returns to the mossy, overgrown aesthetic from before. Nothing interesting other than a tangle of vines at the end and what looks like a bony room.

Chopping down the thick vines with my already suffering needle, I find myself in a small structure. I spot a snail’s shell modelled out of rock, leaning against a wall. The symbol of a dwelling of a Snail Shaman.
The little ghost had an entire collection of charms shaped like that. At the Kingdom’s Edge, when we fought a fierce battle, I saw him wearing them all at the same time. When he defeated me, and became stuck in the Cast-away Shell, I knew he was worthy. And I rescued him.

I saw him fighting The Hollow Knight with those shaman charms on.

My knees buckle as I struggle to stay upright. Eventually, my weakened body feels like collapsing. I fight it, knowing that having to form a cocoon when I wasn’t even in combat would severely hurt my personal pride. I get up, and see a stone bench, like the one the Elderbug used to talk about, assuring an iron bench could also be comfortable. I sit on it, and it does seem quite nice. I focus and set myself to come here if I ever form a cocoon, for now.

I continue upwards, after healing on the bench for a while. I come across a wall of vines, which I slash through. And another. And another.

I look around. This place is a nest. A nest of those green bugs. Expect these ones haven’t hatched yet. I swing my needle at one experimentally. It bursts with a pop, releasing disgusting green slime.

And suddenly, a big one appears. Their mother. And she starts throwing rocks at me.

‘GARAMA!!!!!!!!!!!!’ I scream as I launch into combat. The moss bug charges at me, but I dodge and punish this weak, foolish, helpless bug-

My back breaks as a rock lands on me.
And I can do nothing as this stupid bug mauls me to death.
