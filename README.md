Let me tell you about my Silksong adventure.

I got Silksong a month after its release back in 2025. It was my first metroidvania at the time (I played Silksong before Hollow Knight).
I became hooked onto the game as soon as i started playing. The movement, the combat, it all felt perfect for me.

I was bad at it, though, and put down the game for a month to get good at Mario Kart World.

When I came back to the game, I decided I would attempt to get the True Ending, or delete the game and never touch it again if I failed.
I defeated Lost Lace and got the 'Sister of the Void' achievement around 4 months after that decision.

After 100%ing the game, I felt a crater in my love for this game. I had gotten a respectable time of 2 hours 33 mins IGT Any%, and I have since
defeated Lost Lace hitless. So what else to do?
Well, first of all, play the orignal Hollow Knight, of course. Which I'm doing right now (I'm fairly new, I've just entered Deepnest through Mantis Village).

But then I heard about book writing as a hobby, and I thought that maybe I could channel my experience in the world of Bells and Spiders into writing.
And I added an extra challenge; I would not watch anything relating to Silksong, or even play it through in the writing of this. This is in order for me to do this completely by memory in order to train it, and maybe to tease my knowledge of the lore and route of this game.
